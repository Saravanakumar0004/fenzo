Why FENZO

The FENZO Difference

We don't just manufacture aluminium systems. We engineer living experiences that connect you with the world outside.

Premium Grade Aluminium

6063-T5 aircraft-grade aluminium alloy profiles for unmatched strength-to-weight ratio and corrosion resistance.

Thermal Break Technology

Integrated polyamide thermal breaks reduce heat transfer by up to 70%, keeping interiors comfortable year-round.

Superior Weather Sealing

Triple-chamber EPDM gaskets ensure watertight, dust-free performance in all Indian climate zones.

50+ Powder Coat Finishes

RAL colours, wood-grain laminates, anodised and PVDF coatings — a finish for every architectural vision.

Turnkey Installation

Expert in-house installation teams ensure precise fitting with zero compromise on performance or aesthetics.

10-Year Warranty

Industry-leading warranty on all structural components backed by dedicated post-sale service nationwide.

Acoustic Insulation

Up to 45dB noise reduction, bringing serenity to urban residences, hotels, and commercial spaces.

Enhanced Security

Multi-point locking, anti-lift pins, and toughened glass options ensure the safety of your family and assets.

Pan-India Delivery

Timely delivery and installation across all major cities with a dedicated logistics and project management team.
