Our Journey

Building Dreams
Since 2024

FENZO was founded in 2024 by a team of passionate engineers and architects who believed that India deserved world-class aluminium systems. Starting with a small but determined team in Madurai, we are fast becoming one of Tamil Nadu's most trusted names in premium aluminium fenestration.

Today, our 50,000 sq.ft manufacturing facility houses state-of-the-art CNC fabrication lines, powder-coat ovens, and a dedicated R&D centre — enabling us to serve projects from individual residences to multi-storey commercial towers across India.

2024

Foundation

FENZO Aluminium established in Madurai with an in-house fabrication unit and a vision to deliver premium quality aluminium systems across Tamil Nadu.

2024

First Projects Delivered

Completed our first residential installations in and around Madurai — establishing our reputation for precision, clean finishing, and on-time delivery.

2025

Product Range Expanded

Added folding doors, curtain wall glazing, skylights, and architectural railings — giving clients a complete aluminium solution from a single supplier.

2025

Commercial Projects

Moved beyond homes into showrooms, offices, and institutional buildings — delivering structural glazing and full glass facades.

2026

Growing Across Tamil Nadu

22 projects completed and 7 more under way, with a growing team of skilled fabricators and installers serving residential and commercial clients.
