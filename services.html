What We Offer

End-to-End Solutions

From initial design consultation to final installation and after-sales support — FENZO delivers a seamless, stress-free project experience.

Design Consultation

Our architects and design specialists work closely with you to understand your vision, site constraints, and aesthetic goals. We present detailed CAD drawings and 3D visualisations before production begins.

  • ✓ Site survey & measurement
  • ✓ CAD drawings & 3D renders
  • ✓ Material & colour selection
  • ✓ Budget estimation

Precision Fabrication

Our CNC-equipped fabrication facility in Madurai processes premium aluminium profiles from global systems brands. Every component is cut, drilled, and assembled under strict quality controls.

  • ✓ CNC precision cutting
  • ✓ European profile systems
  • ✓ Thermal break assembly
  • ✓ Quality inspection at every stage

Expert Installation

Our trained installation crews handle projects of all scales — from single-apartment upgrades to full high-rise curtain wall cladding. We ensure plumb, level, weathertight completion every time.

  • ✓ Certified installation teams
  • ✓ Waterproofing & sealing
  • ✓ Site safety compliance
  • ✓ Post-installation inspection

Glass Works

We supply and fit premium glass solutions including double-glazed IGUs, toughened safety glass, laminated glass, solar-control coatings, and smart privacy glass for all our aluminium systems.

  • ✓ Double & triple glazing
  • ✓ Solar-control coatings
  • ✓ Laminated safety glass
  • ✓ Smart/privacy glass

After-Sales Support

Our relationship doesn't end at handover. FENZO provides comprehensive post-installation service including annual maintenance contracts, spare parts supply, hardware adjustments, and seal replacement.

  • ✓ 5-year workmanship warranty
  • ✓ Annual maintenance contracts
  • ✓ Genuine spare parts
  • ✓ Emergency repair service

Facade Consultancy

For large commercial and institutional projects, our facade consultancy service covers structural analysis, wind-load calculations, thermal performance modelling, and compliance with local building codes.

  • ✓ Structural load analysis
  • ✓ Thermal & acoustic modelling
  • ✓ Building code compliance
  • ✓ LEED/green building support

How We Work

Our Process

A clear, structured approach that keeps your project on time and on budget — from first enquiry to final handover.

01

Enquiry & Site Visit

Contact us and share your project details. Our team visits your site to take precise measurements and assess requirements.

02

Design & Quotation

We prepare CAD drawings and a detailed itemised quotation. You select finishes and glass specifications from our range.

03

Fabrication

Upon order confirmation, precision fabrication begins at our Madurai facility. You receive production milestone updates.

04

Installation & Handover

Our certified team installs, seals, and tests every unit. We issue warranty documentation upon final inspection sign-off.
