Surface Treatments

Colours & Finishes

Over 20 standard finishes — powder coated, anodised, and wood grain — with custom RAL colours available on request.

Powder Coating

Electrostatically applied thermosetting polymer coating. Superb UV resistance, chip resistance, and the widest colour range — any RAL shade available.

UV Stable Custom RAL Matt / Gloss / Satin

Anodising

Electrochemical oxidation that penetrates the aluminium surface to create an integral hard oxide layer. Exceptional durability, scratch resistance, and coastal corrosion protection.

Coastal Grade 25µm Thickness Metallic Sheen

Wood Grain

Sublimation-applied wood-look laminate over powder-coated aluminium profiles. The warmth of timber aesthetics with the durability of aluminium — no warping, rot, or termites.

Timber Aesthetic Zero Maintenance 5 Species

Glass Specification

Glass Options

The right glass specification enhances comfort, safety, and energy efficiency — ask our team for a tailored recommendation.

🪟

Single Glazing

4mm or 6mm clear / tinted float glass. Cost-effective for dry climates and interior applications.

🔲

Double Glazing (IGU)

5mm + 12mm air gap + 5mm. Superior thermal insulation and noise reduction — recommended for Madurai climate.

☀️

Solar Control

Low-E or reflective coated glass blocks 60–70% solar heat gain while maintaining visible light transmission.

🛡️

Safety Glass

Toughened (tempered) or laminated glass for balconies, floor-to-ceiling panels, and safety-critical applications.
